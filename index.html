How a visit flows through HHMS

  1. RegisterReception adds the patient details.
  2. BookAn appointment slot is fixed with a doctor.
  3. ConsultThe doctor sees the patient and notes vitals.
  4. RecordDiagnosis and prescription are saved.
  5. BillAn invoice is generated and paid.
